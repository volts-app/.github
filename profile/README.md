## The product

VOLT is a social live-streaming platform where creators go live, talk with their audience in real time, team up with other streamers, and earn from the support of their community.

We are currently in the **store publishing phase**, preparing the iOS and Android apps for release on the **App Store** and **Google Play**. The public website is already live at **[voltsapp.com](https://voltsapp.com)** — it is the home of the live app, and the best place to explore what VOLT does before the store listings go live.

## What you can do on VOLT

| Area | Highlights |
| --- | --- |
| **Go live** | Crystal-clear live audio, low-latency multi-party video, screen sharing, and real-time audience interaction. |
| **Battle & collaborate** | PK battles between streamers, co-hosted rooms, live leaderboards, and audience-driven scoreboards. |
| **Grow a community** | Follow creators, browse a feed of reels and posts, comment, like, repost, and chat one-to-one. |
| **Support creators** | Send virtual gifts during streams and chats, and climb gift leaderboards. |
| **Creator earnings** | Track earnings and request fast payouts through creator wallet tools. |
| **Payments anywhere** | Top up and pay with major fiat currencies or crypto, on the web and through in-app purchases. |
| **Safe by design** | Reporting, moderation tools, community guidelines, and admin review across streams, posts, and accounts. |

## Engineering

VOLT is built and operated by a small product team. Our source repositories are **private**; this organization page is the public home for the product, release updates, and the documentation we choose to share.

At a high level, the platform is built on TypeScript and Node.js services, real-time streaming and messaging infrastructure, MongoDB and Redis, and integrations for payments, payouts, media, and push notifications.
